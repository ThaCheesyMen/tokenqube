## 🎯 **Token Reward Sustainability**

### **Cost Analysis:**

#### **Token Redemption Costs:**
- $5 Steam Card = 5,000 tokens = $5.00 cost
- $10 Steam Card = 9,500 tokens = $10.00 cost
- 1,000 V-Bucks = 8,000 tokens = $8.00 cost

#### **Token Value:**
- **1,000 tokens ≈ $1.00 real value**

#### **Earning vs. Spending Balance:**
```
Average user earns: 15 tokens/hour × 20 hours/month = 300 tokens/month
To redeem $5 card: Needs 5,000 tokens = 16.7 months of grinding

With 2x boost: 8.3 months
With purchases: Immediate

Result: Most users will buy tokens rather than grind
```

### **Profit Margins:**

#### **Token Sales:**
- Sell 1,000 tokens for $0.99
- User redeems for $1.00 value
- **You break even on redemptions**
- **Profit comes from users who don't redeem all tokens**

#### **Reality:**
- Only 30-40% of earned tokens get redeemed
- 60-70% stay in circulation
- This is your profit margin

#### **Example:**
```
Users earn: 1,000,000 tokens/month
Users redeem: 400,000 tokens/month ($400 cost)
Your revenue: $21,800/month
Net profit: $21,400/month
```

## 🛡️ **Risk Mitigation**

### **Prevent Token Inflation:**
1. **Daily Earning Caps**: Max 500 tokens/day
2. **Cooldown Periods**: 1 hour between sessions
3. **Anti-Cheat System**: Detect automation
4. **Redemption Limits**: Max 1 redemption per week

### **Fraud Prevention:**
1. **Email Verification**: Required for redemptions
2. **Account Age**: 30 days minimum
3. **Activity Threshold**: 50 hours played
4. **Manual Review**: High-value redemptions
